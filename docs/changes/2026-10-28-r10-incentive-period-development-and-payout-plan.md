# Incentive period report and payout plan

Implemented the R10-inspired employee incentive summary within Employee Commission Report. The new default incentive-period view counts earned commission by recorded completion date and pending commission for jobs whose job date is within the selected range. This follows the user's decision to show only jobs started in the selected month as pending. The previous job-date view remains selectable. EOM eligible means earned in a full selected month; it does not mean paid. Earned records without a completion timestamp cannot be assigned to an EOM period and are excluded from that view.

Employee rows now present employee number/name, designation, job count, EOM eligible, pending, and total incentive separately. Expanding an employee loads paginated job-level totals from the Reporting module, so multiple commission entries on the same job appear as one job row. Job rows link to the job and show job date, last earned date, customer/vehicle, eligible, pending, and total amounts. The original entry-level breakdown is still available through a secondary action. The summary export uses the same employee groups and period rule, with existing report export formats and permissions. The new job endpoint uses the same tenant and organization scoped commission query and Reports View permission.

No actual employee payment, payroll, or settlement status was added. Current Payment payee handling is oriented around customer and supplier documents, and the existing reporting capability explicitly says payroll is unavailable. The recommended next feature is an Incentive Payout workflow in the HR/payroll owner: prepare a monthly batch from eligible commission sources, snapshot source amounts and completion dates, review and approve, then record a real outbound employee disbursement through a Payment-module extension with an employee payee and accounting policy. Track allocations to each incentive source, prevent duplicate settlement with transactional locking and source uniqueness, and derive unpaid/partially paid/paid from posted disbursements. Reversals and corrections should append audit entries rather than rewrite approved history. Keep salary, deductions, statutory payroll, and general payroll separate until their rules are specified. The user flow should be employee summary > inspect jobs > prepare payout > review/approve > record payment > see status and receipt.
