# R10 incentive summary and Employee Commission report plan

Reviewed the one-page scanned R10 Incentive Summary, current Employee Commission UI, reporting API/service, filters, tests, and recent reporting change records. No application code was changed.

R10 shows a date range, service station/location, employee ID and name, Incentive Amt., Incentive Amt. (EOM), a location subtotal, and a grand total. Its sample has the same amount in both incentive columns, but this does not establish the meaning of EOM or a calculation rule. It has no job-level detail.

The existing Employee Commission report already has employee summary rows, employee number, designation, distinct job count, total/earned/pending commission, and inline expansion to paginated Vehicle Service commission entries. It also has a date range, employee lookup, status filters, and exports. The expanded entries are per assignment/source, so a job can appear multiple times. Its backend is scoped to the active organization unit and derives commission amounts from stored Vehicle Service assignments and supervisor commission, excluding cancelled amounts from active totals. There is no verified end-of-month payroll/settlement source in this report.

Proposed development: keep this report and its existing canonical commission calculation. Make employee summary the primary view with employee number/name, job count, earned, pending, and total incentive, plus the selected date range and verified organization-unit label. Expand one employee directly under that row. Add a backend job-level projection over the same report query, grouped by employee and job, to show one job row with job number/link, date, customer/vehicle, earned, pending, and total incentive. Preserve the assignment-level breakdown through a secondary expansion or the existing detailed view. Server-side pagination, filter consistency, tenant/organization authorization, and totals must remain correct. Use compact responsive cards on mobile and keep advanced filters collapsed.

Do not equate R10's EOM amount with total/earned commission without its business definition and authoritative source. Confirm what EOM measures before planning that separate column. Do not label commission as paid: the current report has pending/earned/cancelled states but no commission payroll settlement record. Confirm whether R10's location is equivalent to AutoERP's organization unit before labeling it as such. Validate job totals against employee and grand totals, including multiple assignments per job, supervisor entries, cancelled rows, filters, pagination, and export output.
