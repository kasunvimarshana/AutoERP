# Physical five-wash card review

Reviewed the two supplied card photos and the current Promotion module without changing application code or data. The reverse has five Date rows with eight date-digit boxes per row and one Free Issued Date row. The current count-offer form already accepts five paid qualifying units and one free reward, and an earned offer can be included in a vehicle-bound card design.

The present rule counts charged, posted invoice-line quantity, begins only after card/offer assignment, and repeats every five paid units while active. It does not import stamps already written on a physical card, cap an earned card at one completed cycle, or show a five-mark card view. Card designs also require an end date. Those differences need business confirmation before changing the Promotion module, especially whether a mark represents a separate paid wash visit, whether the card ends after one free wash, and whether previously stamped visits must be entered.
