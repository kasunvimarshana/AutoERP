# Vehicle Rental business decisions required

This register contains financially or operationally material rules that were not proven by the supplied legacy videos or the current authoritative source. They must not be implemented as hidden defaults.

| Decision | Required approval |
|---|---|
| Partial monthly charge: exact calendar days, fixed 30 days, full started cycle, or another rule | Rental Operations + Finance |
| Included-KM pooling period and replacement behavior | Rental Operations |
| Category-only confirmed reservation capacity guarantee | Rental Operations + Sales |
| Replacement vehicle customer rate and owner payable split | Rental Operations + Finance |
| Downtime/off-road charging or deduction | Rental Operations + Legal |
| Garage/internal mileage billing/payable policy | Rental Operations |
| Fuel, repair, damage, accident and insurance-excess responsibility | Rental Operations + Legal + Finance |
| Security-deposit application priority and approval | Finance + Rental Operations |
| Early termination, suspension, extension and renewal | Legal + Rental Operations |
| Multiple-driver salary/OT/night-out split | HR + Rental Operations |
| Holiday, double-OT and triple-OT definitions | HR + Payroll |
| Foreign-currency and exchange-rate dates | Finance |
| Tax calculation order and rounding | Tax + Finance |
| Customer credit exposure formula, enforcement point and override | Credit Control + Finance |
| Whether one physical Running Chart may split across agreements | Rental Operations |
| Whether Vehicle Finance is approved product scope or only lessor/supply relationships are required | Product + Finance |
| Mandatory legal documents and blocking stages | Compliance + Rental Operations |
| Mandatory custody photos, signatures and documents | Legal + Rental Operations |

Each approved decision must record the selected rule, approver, effective date, tenant/organization scope, worked examples, configuration source, affected agreements, and acceptance tests.
